    Tuning History¶

    The first target was an RTP of 96% with high volatility and a good feel in play. Version 2.0.0 got a target RTP of 96.20%. The subsequent versions, to version 2.5.0, have the same target. Each iteration used the exact calculator first and the simulator second.

    Version 2.0.0: the redesign¶

    Iteration 6 changed the structure of the game. These were the steps.

    1. New reel rules. The gap between special symbols increased from 2 to 3. The design got a limit of 3 AUS symbols in sequence. A new generator makes strips that obey the two rules.
    2. KEY symbols. The base game got one KEY symbol on each of reels 1, 2 and 3 (before: one KEY on reel 3). The mean number of KEY symbols for each base spin increased from 0.04 to 0.12.
    3. First result. With no other change, the RTP was 105.6%. The base collect was 62.6%, and the kraken gave 48% of this. The free spins gave 8.6%.
    4. Free spins trigger. The target was 1 in 175. Whole FS symbols on strips of 100 symbols cannot give this value. The selected layout has FS symbols on all 5 reels (3, 2, 2, 2, 2) and gives 1 in 171.66.
    5. Levels. With one KEY symbol on each of bonus reels 1 to 3, levels 2, 3 and 4 came in 12%, 1.8% and 0.3% of the free spins. With 2 / 2 / 1 KEY symbols on bonus reels 1 / 2 / 3, they come in 34%, 13% and 6%. This is almost the same as in version 1.1.0. The thresholds did not change.
    6. Golden kraken. The weights of the two kraken values changed from 2 : 1 to 8 : 1, in the base game and in the free spins. The mean kraken value is now 666.7 credits (before: 1000). The result was an RTP of 101.9%.
    7. Balance. The base reel strips lost one FISH3 symbol and five FISH2 symbols, and the base weights of FISH2 and FISH3 moved to the low values. The paytable and the FISH symbols of the bonus reel strips did not change. The base weights of FISH1 are the fine adjustment: their sum is 10,000, so that the RTP is 96.199971%.

    Version 2.1.0: reels that are different from each other¶

    In version 2.0.0, the five reels of a reel set were almost the same. Each PIC symbol had the same number of symbols on each reel strip. Each AUS symbol had almost the same number. Version 2.1.0 gives each PIC and AUS symbol more symbols on some reels and fewer symbols on other reels. The page Reel strips shows the pattern of each symbol.

    Patterns. Each symbol got its own pattern. For example, PIC1 has most of its symbols on reels 1 and 2, and PIC2 has most of its symbols on reels 4 and 5.

    Special symbols. The number of each special symbol on each reel did not change. Thus the trigger frequency, the KEY frequency and the level probabilities did not change.

    Strength. The exact calculator and the simulator gave these results for three strengths, before the balance:

    Strength Change of the symbol counts RTP Ways pays Collect Free spins Base spins with a win

    Mild 20% or less 95.34% 35.43% 39.15% 20.76% 30.1%
    Medium 40% or less 92.56% 32.90% 39.15% 20.51% 31.2%
    Strong 60% or less 88.40% 29.58% 39.15% 19.67% 27.0%

    The column "Base spins with a win" comes from simulations of 4,000,000,000 rounds. The medium strength was selected. This column depends also on the arrangement of the symbols on each strip, not only on the strength (see version 2.3.0).

    Cause of the lower RTP. A way of 5 symbols needs the symbol or a WILD symbol on each reel. The expected number of ways is the product of the mean counts of the reels. When the counts are not equal, the product is smaller. With the medium strength, the RTP of the 5 OAK pays in the base game decreased from 17.43% to 14.84%.

    Balance. The RTP went back into the ways pays. The 5 OAK pay of each pay symbol increased. The 3 OAK pays and the 4 OAK pays did not change. Long wins are less frequent than before, and they pay more. The RTP of the 5 OAK pays in the base game is now 18.13%.

    Version 2.2.0: fish values that decrease with the level¶

    In version 2.1.0, the weight tables of the free spins became better with each level. Version 2.2.0 turns this around. Level 1 has the best fish values. On levels 2 to 4, the high values are harder to get with each level. The multipliers of the levels did not change.

    Levels 2 to 4. The weight tables of FISH1, FISH2 and FISH3 became worse with each level. The step is the same as the step that made them better in version 2.1.0. Each value keeps a weight of 1 or more, so each value stays possible on each level. The golden kraken has weights of 16 : 1, 32 : 1, 64 : 1 on levels 2, 3 and 4 (before: 8 : 1 on each level).

    RTP that these changes free. The exact calculator gave these results for changes of levels 2 to 4 only:

    Weight tables of levels 2 to 4 Free spins RTP Total RTP Free RTP
    Version 2.1.0: better with each level 20.84% 96.20% -
    The level 1 tables on all levels 20.25% 95.61% 0.59%
    FISH1 to FISH3 worse with each level 19.69% 95.04% 1.16%
    FISH1 to FISH3 worse with each level, and fewer golden krakens with each level 18.89% 94.25% 1.95%
    The largest possible change: the lowest value of FISH1 to FISH3 and no golden kraken 17.29% 92.65% 3.56%

    The fourth row was selected. With a weight of 1 or more for each value, it frees 1.94% RTP. The limit is low because the weight tables control only a part of the RTP of levels 2 to 4. In version 2.1.0, levels 2 to 4 gave 12.65% RTP: 2.69% from ways pays, 4.68% from the kraken and 5.28% from FISH1 to FISH3.

    Level 1. The free RTP went to level 1, to FISH3 and to the kraken. FISH1 and FISH2 did not change on level 1.

    • The FISH3 weights changed from 60 / 25 / 10 / 5 to 43 / 19 / 25 / 13. The two high values have 38% of the weight (before: 15%).
    • The golden kraken weights changed from 8 : 1 to 2 : 1. The golden kraken has 33% of the weight (before: 11%).

    Two other options for level 1 were calculated. They were not selected.

    • All free RTP in the two high values of FISH1, FISH2 and FISH3: their weights must increase by a factor of 46. Then the two high values have 89% to 96% of the weight, and the two low values almost do not show.
    • The two high values of FISH1, FISH2 and FISH3 with a factor of 4.7, and golden kraken weights of 3 : 1.

    The base game is the same as in version 2.4.0. The wins of the free spins are almost the same. The difference is what the player sees: PIC1 on the first two reels more frequently.

    Design decisions¶

    One KEY symbol on each of base reels 1, 2 and 3. A KEY symbol lands 1 in 8.7 base spins. Two or three KEY symbols can land in one spin. Then each KEY symbol collects each FISH value.

    The kraken stays on the base reel strips. The KEY anticipation on reels 4 and 5 has a purpose only if the large fish are there. The kraken gives 32% RTP in the base game. A KEY symbol and the kraken land together 1 in 217 base spins.

    Golden kraken weights 8 : 1 in the base game. With weights 2 : 1, the kraken gave 48% RTP in the base game, and the total was too high. The golden kraken is now less frequent in the base game. The usual kraken pays 500 credits. The golden kraken pays 2000 credits.

    The balance of version 2.0.0 came from the small base fish. In that version, the paytable and the free spins kept their value. The free spins were less frequent than before, so their value for each trigger did not decrease.

    The balance of version 2.1.0 came from the 5 OAK pays. The reel strips with patterns give fewer long ways. Larger 5 OAK pays put this RTP back into the ways pays, so that the RTP of each component stays almost the same.

    2 / 2 / 1 KEY symbols on bonus reels 1 / 2 / 3. This gives 0.20 KEY symbols for each free spin, the same as in version 1.1.0. The levels keep their frequency.

    PIC1 is rare and pays the most. PIC1 has 7 / 2 / 4 / 6 / 5 symbols on base reels 1 to 5. A PIC1 win needs reel 2, where PIC1 has few symbols. The pays of PIC1 are 5 / 20 / 80 credits for 3 / 4 / 5 OAK.

    Eight spins for levels 2 to 4. With 10 spins, level 4 was too frequent.

    Bonus reels with more PIC symbols. The bonus reel strips have no WILD symbol. More PIC symbols give more ways pays in the free spins.

    The best fish values are on level 1. Each free spins feature plays level 1. Thus each feature has a chance of the high FISH3 values and of the golden kraken. On the higher levels, the multiplier gives the large wins, and the high values are harder to get. The mean FISH3 value decreases from 131.0 credits on level 1 to 63.0 credits on level 4. The golden kraken has weights of 2 : 1 on level 1 and 64 : 1 on level 4.

    Possible subsequent changes¶

    • A variant with the kraken only in the free spins: remove FISH4 from the base reel strips and put that RTP into the free spins (mean free spins win approximately 80 x bet).
    • A higher volatility variant: decrease the trigger frequency and increase the level multipliers.
    • A win limit, if the market requires one.
