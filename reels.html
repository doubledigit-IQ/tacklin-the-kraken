    Reel Strips¶

    Requirements¶

    No. Requirement Check
    1 Each reel strip has 100 symbols. Generator, engine, workbook
    2 There are 3 or more PIC/AUS symbols between two special symbols (FISH1 to FISH4, KEY, WILD, FS). The strip is a loop. Generator, engine, workbook
    3 There are never more than 3 AUS symbols in sequence. This applies to all AUS symbols together, not only to stacks of one AUS symbol. Generator, engine, workbook
    4 Reel 1 has no WILD symbol. Generator, engine, workbook
    5 KEY is on reels 1, 2 and 3. Each of these base reel strips has one KEY symbol. Generator, engine, workbook
    6 FISH3 is only on reel 4. FISH4 is only on reel 5. Generator, engine, workbook
    7 The bonus reel strips have no WILD symbol and no FS symbol. Generator, engine, workbook

    Requirements 4 and 7 are also conditions of the math model. See Base game math.

    Results of the requirements¶

    • Requirement 2 has this result: a 4-row window of one reel shows one special symbol at most. Thus one reel shows one KEY symbol, one FS symbol or one FISH symbol at most.
    • Requirement 3 has this result: each 4-row window of each reel shows one or more symbols that are not AUS symbols.
    • The script tools/verify_all.py examines each window of each reel strip for these two results.

    Reel strip generator¶

    The script gamemath/gen_reels.py makes the reel strips from a recipe. The recipe gives the number of each symbol on each reel. The generator uses a fixed seed (667). Thus the result is repeatable. A different seed gives a different arrangement of the same symbols. The arrangement changes the RTP of the ways pays a little, and it changes the number of spins with a win. See Tuning history.

    The generator does these steps for each reel:

    1. It puts the special symbols in a random sequence. The spaces between them are the spans.
    2. It makes stacks of PIC symbols and puts them into the spans. A stack has 3 symbols or less.
    3. It makes groups of 1 to 3 AUS symbols. It puts one group at most into each position between two PIC stacks or adjacent to a special symbol. Thus two AUS groups never touch.
    4. It gives AUS groups first to the spans that have fewer than 3 pay symbols.
    5. It makes sure that the strip obeys all requirements. If not, it starts again.

    Symbol weighting on each reel¶

    Each PIC symbol and each AUS symbol has its own pattern. It has more symbols on some reels and fewer symbols on other reels. Thus the reels are different from each other.

    Symbol Pattern Base reel strips: symbols on reels 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 Bonus reel strips: symbols on reels 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5
    PIC1 Most symbols on reels 1, 4 and 5 8 / 2 / 2 / 6 / 6 12 / 4 / 4 / 11 / 10
    PIC2 Most symbols on the last reels 4 / 5 / 5 / 7 / 9 5 / 8 / 9 / 11 / 12
    PIC3 Most symbols on the middle reels 5 / 8 / 9 / 8 / 5 7 / 11 / 14 / 11 / 7
    PIC4 Most symbols on the outer reels 9 / 7 / 4 / 6 / 10 14 / 10 / 6 / 10 / 14
    PIC5 Most symbols on reels 1 and 3 11 / 5 / 10 / 5 / 8 17 / 8 / 16 / 7 / 12
    AUS1 Most symbols on the last reels 7 / 11 / 11 / 14 / 16 6 / 11 / 14 / 15 / 15
    AUS2 Most symbols on the first reels 16 / 14 / 11 / 10 / 7 14 / 15 / 13 / 8 / 6
    AUS3 Most symbols on reels 2 and 4 7 / 17 / 6 / 15 / 10 5 / 13 / 6 / 9 / 6
    AUS4 Most symbols on reels 1 and 3 16 / 7 / 16 / 7 / 10 2 / 2 / 3 / 2 / 2 (no pattern)
    AUS5 Most symbols on the middle reels 8 / 13 / 16 / 12 / 9 1 / 1 / 3 / 1 / 2 (no pattern)

    The bold numbers are the reels that the pattern favours.

    • When a symbol has most of its symbols on the first reels, a win of this symbol starts frequently. Few of these wins continue to reel 5.
    • When a symbol has most of its symbols on the last reels, a win of this symbol starts less frequently. More of these wins continue to reel 5.
    • PIC1 has few symbols on reels 2 and 3. Thus a PIC1 win starts rarely. PIC1 has the largest pays.
    • On the bonus reel strips, AUS4 and AUS5 have 3 symbols or fewer on each reel. They have no pattern there.
    • The special symbols have no pattern. The requirements and the math model give their numbers.

    The patterns came with version 2.1.0. Before that version, each PIC symbol had the same number of symbols on each reel of a reel set. The pattern of PIC1 changed with version 2.3.0. See Tuning history.
