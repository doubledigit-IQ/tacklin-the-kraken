    Tacklin' the Kraken - Game Math Document¶

    Item Value
    Game Tacklin' the Kraken
    Math version 2.0.0
    Reel window 5 reels x 4 rows
    Pay method 1024 ways, left to right
    Bet 10 credits
    Theoretical RTP 96.2000%
    Volatility Medium to high (standard deviation 10.17 x bet for each round)

    Purpose¶

    This document gives the rules, the configuration and the mathematics of the game. It also gives the results of the verification.

    How to read this document¶

    • All wins are in credits. The bet is 10 credits.
    • "x bet" means a multiple of the bet.
    • A symbol name in capital letters (for example KEY) is a technical name. The glossary gives the definitions.
    • KEY is the technical name of the collect symbol. The playable game shows this symbol as a fishing hook and uses the name HOOK.
    • The tables come from the configuration file. A script writes them. Thus the numbers agree with the game.

    Writing standard¶

    This document uses the writing rules of ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English) for most of the text. The sentences are short. The verbs are in the active voice and in simple tenses. One word has one meaning. Full compliance is not a goal of this issue. The tables, the formulas and the game names are outside the standard.

    Assumptions¶

    The game design did not specify these items. This math model uses the values that follow. You can change each value in the configuration.

    Item Value in this model
    Pay method Ways pays (1024 ways). Lines are not used.
    Units of the paytable and of the fish values Credits, at a bet of 10 credits
    Spins for levels 2, 3 and 4 8, 8, 8
    Level multiplier Applies to all wins (ways pays and fish collect)
    FS symbols on the bonus reels None. The free spins have no retrigger.
    Win limit None
    Target RTP 96.20%
